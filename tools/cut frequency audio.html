Low-Pass Audio Cutter BiquadFilter

Load an audio file, choose a cutoff, and remove everything above that frequency. Preview in real-time and optionally render a processed WAV for download.

Works entirely in your browser. Your audio never leaves your device.

Use the play/pause and scrub bar as usual.

How it works

This page uses the Web Audio API. For preview, your audio element is routed through a BiquadFilterNode set to lowpass. For export, the file is processed in an OfflineAudioContext and saved as a 16-bit PCM WAV.
