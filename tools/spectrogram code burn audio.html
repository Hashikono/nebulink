Spectrogram tips
  • In Audacity: Track ▸ Spectrogram ▸ Spectrogram Settings… set FFT 4096, Hann, and show the band you chose (e.g., 14–18 kHz).
  • If you hear the tones, raise fmin/fmax or lower Strength.
  • For very long audio, the mask canvas can be wide—zoom out or shrink the browser width; the audio watermark still spans the full duration.
