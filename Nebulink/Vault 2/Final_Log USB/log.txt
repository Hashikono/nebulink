I lied about my last report.
The array just detected something that I have to investigate before the “thing” fixes itself again. 

This may actually be the operation’s final log…
To that accord, I entrust the security of this information to the one who found out about...us

For as long as I can remember, I’ve been stuck in this “new world”, or at least that’s what I’ve always referred to it as. At around the age of, ██ I started studying stars after I received a smuggled book from the doctor next door. One day, I decided to look out into Eridanus at night. It was a beautiful constellation that formed a small river of stars, but something weird happened...

At exactly 2:22 AM, the spot where Eridanus is meant to be formed something resembling a dark circle, as if someone punched a hole in the night sky. For the next few months, I studied this phenomenon and in 1989, I formed the group you are probably well familiar with, known as Operation DS (Dark Sky). Using some smuggled technology, our members formulated what we refer to as the “array” (sky analyzer). It was almost destroyed when “kryptoss”’s house was ransacked, but a little repairing did the trick.

I’m so glad we live near the frontiers of this barren place, and we only had 2 CCTV cameras stationed in our little abode.

“uxynnek” with my guidance, ended up developing the star terminal, or what the world has come to terms with as Nebulink...a system that we devised to store the array’s logs and detections. It started finding weird patterns in the sky, from curved stars to...static??? Later on, it detected some electricity emitting somewhere from “space” but the oddity of it all came from its movement, as if it were in a circuit...

The audio files also gave us weird images when put into the spectrogram.

Nearing the beginning of 1991, some of our members started disappearing, and I'm getting signs that the government is on our heels.

At the time of writing this log, I am the LAST member of this operation.
After adjusting a few parameters, the array finally caught what I’ve always suspected...the sky was a DEVICE. 
Eridanus was a bug in their system. 
The government is a lie.
My time is near...

I was going through the city one day in search of replacement parts when I caught sight of a propaganda poster about the avoidance of some sort of new technology. It was “Nebulink”...

I am going to start my exploration in about an hour to find out the truth about this whole place.

“norahs” also hastily gave me the USB drive/key with our operation’s usual password “1984” before she ran away...I later found out she was ███████████████ by some ███████████████.
